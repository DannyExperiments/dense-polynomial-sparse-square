# Changes from the reviewed manuscript

The new manuscript retains the local systems, the deterministic list of periods, the scaling parameters, the repetition count and the witness from the archived proof. It separates the auxiliary dense polynomial and the endpoint argument into numbered results. The endpoint proof now specifies the relevant index and both closed intervals explicitly. The certificate section spells out the half-open interpolation intervals, including the rule for a term at a knot.

The proof of the stronger constant now supplies a short rational comparison from the archived coarse estimates as well as the certificate check. The paper distinguishes the coarse support bound checked in Lean from the tighter upper bound computed using the recorded local factors. A final corollary isolates the geometric sequence of degrees. The provenance paragraph records the scope of the independent AI audit and current formalization.
